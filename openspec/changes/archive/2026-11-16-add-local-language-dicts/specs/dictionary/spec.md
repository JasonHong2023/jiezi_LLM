## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Language Preference

同一個詞在多本辭典都有時，問題提到台語（台語、臺語、閩南語、台灣話、台羅）SHALL 先用臺灣閩南語辭典，提到客語（客語、客家話）SHALL 先用客家語辭典，否則 SHALL 先用國語辭典；例句的對照標記 SHALL 只做格式轉換，不改詞條內容。
When several dictionaries hold a word, Taiwanese questions SHALL prefer the Taiwanese dictionary, Hakka questions the Hakka one, and others the Mandarin one; interlinear markers SHALL only be reformatted.

#### Scenario: 問台語 / Asking about Taiwanese

- **WHEN** 使用者問「台語的『歹勢』是什麼意思？」
  The user asks what 歹勢 means in Taiwanese
- **THEN** 附上臺灣閩南語辭典的詞條（含台羅 pháinn-sè），出處為教育部《臺灣閩南語常用詞辭典》
  The Taiwanese entry with its Tâi-lô reading is attached with its attribution
